What it changes

Four things the site can't do today.

Out-of-hours enquiries stop evaporating

Every visitor gets an answer at the moment of interest instead of a form and a wait, whatever the hour or timezone.

Demos arrive pre-qualified

Portfolio size and requirements are captured in the conversation, so the first call starts with context rather than discovery.

First-time-fix rates get a head start

Model, fault and photo are captured before dispatch, which is exactly the information an engineer needs to arrive with the right part.

Every interaction feeds the data engine

Conversations, bookings and resolutions land in the same record that already powers Iterum IQ reporting.
