The always-on layer · concept

Your office closes.
Your portfolio doesn't.

Appliances fail at 10 PM and asset managers compare suppliers on a Sunday. This concept gives both of them an answer in seconds.

Demos book themselves

An operator lands on the site at 9 PM, asks about portfolio coverage, and walks away with a confirmed demo slot. No form, no wait, no lost lead.

Residents report issues any hour

A broken washing machine gets reported, triaged and booked for an engineer visit in one conversation, with the property manager notified automatically.

Every conversation feeds the data engine

Issues, bookings and follow-ups land in one place, so the reporting your clients already value gets richer with every interaction.

First-time-fix starts in the chat

Make, model and a photo of the fault are captured before an engineer is ever dispatched, so the right part arrives on the first visit.
